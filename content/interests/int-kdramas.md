Edward enjoys Korean dramas. His favourites:

- **Start-Up**: young founders chasing their dreams in Korea's version of Silicon Valley.
- **Lovely Runner**: a fan travels back in time to save her favourite star.
- **The Glory**: a gripping revenge thriller.
- **Hospital Playlist**: five doctors who have been friends since medical school, and their band.
- **Twenty-Five Twenty-One**: a coming-of-age story about a young fencer in the late 1990s.
