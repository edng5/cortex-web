Edward started playing chess in 2024 after watching *The Queen's Gambit*, and plays on chess.com.

## Favourite openings
- **Ruy Lopez** (as White): one of the oldest and most classical openings, 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5.
- **Caro-Kann Defence** (as Black): a solid reply to 1.e4, with 1...c6.
