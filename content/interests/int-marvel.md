Edward enjoys watching Marvel. His favourite superheroes are **Iron Man** and **Spider-Man**, and
during the COVID pandemic he watched every MCU movie from Phase 1 through Phase 4.

His fandom also inspired The Watcher, a Gemini-powered tool that analyzes MCU transcripts to
recommend which of the 34 movies and 11 shows are essential to the main story.
