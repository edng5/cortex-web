Edward is into cars, especially performance cars. His favourites:

- **BMW M4**: BMW's twin-turbo inline-six sports coupe.
- **Nissan GT-R**: the all-wheel-drive "Godzilla", famous for its twin-turbo V6.
- **Toyota Supra**: a Japanese icon with a long tuning legacy, revived as the GR Supra.
