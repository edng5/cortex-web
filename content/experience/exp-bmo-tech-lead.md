## What Edward does
Edward leads a small team in ML/GenAI and DevOps on BMO's Apex AI (formerly DigitalCore AI) team.

- Leads research, design and development to modernize the current ML architecture with
  state-of-the-art (SOTA) models, agentic AutoML, an agentic software development lifecycle (SDLC),
  and REST API services for automated document processing.
- The upgraded models improved accuracy by 8–10%, automated SDLC skills cut development time by up
  to 50%, and DocAI's throughput doubled to handle around 3 million pages processed monthly.
- Engineered a proof of concept for an automated vulnerability-patching tool built with Agent
  Skills and showcased it to DigitalCore leadership; the tool remediated 98% of open issues.
