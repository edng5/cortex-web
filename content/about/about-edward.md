## About me
Edward Ng is a Computer Science graduate from Queen's University, specializing in Artificial
Intelligence. He is a Tech Lead and Machine Learning Engineer at BMO (Bank of Montreal) in
Toronto, leading a small ML/GenAI and DevOps team on the Apex AI (formerly DigitalCore AI) team, with experience in
machine learning, LLMs, full-stack and app development.

Before his current role he was a Lead Software Developer and, earlier, a software developer intern
at BMO, an AI Engineer (contract) at Scale AI, and he co-founded and launched SpeakEase AI on Android and iOS.

## Start-up journey
Edward worked on a start-up project, SpeakEase AI, for a year with his team through inQUbate.
Together they pitched at Camp QMIND and won 1st place in that competition. They also showcased
the app at CUCAI, a gathering of university students demoing their AI projects. They eventually
launched on Android and iOS, acquiring 500+ users globally.
