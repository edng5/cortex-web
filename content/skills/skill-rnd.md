Edward works across research and development. As Tech Lead and Machine Learning Engineer at BMO he
leads the research, design and development of a modernized ML architecture for automated document
processing, adopting state-of-the-art models, agentic AutoML and an agentic SDLC; the upgraded
models improved accuracy by 8–10%. His own research projects include Mind-to-Speech, which decodes
imagined speech from EEG signals with CNN+LSTM and CNN+Transformer models (83.33% accuracy), and
NEOS, a neuroevolution experiment using the NEAT algorithm. He built proofs of concept such as an
automated vulnerability-patching tool with Agent Skills, and won 1st place in the QMIND AI
Competition (2023) at Queen's. His publications are a work in progress.
