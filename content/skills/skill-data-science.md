- **DocVision:** NumPy drives the scan-noise pipeline and the metrics (confusion matrices,
  latency percentiles), pandas builds the per-class and per-field result tables, and Matplotlib
  draws the architecture diagram, predicted-field overlays, confusion matrices and training curves.
- **Mind-to-Speech:** NumPy holds and filters the EEG signals and turns them into topographic maps,
  which Matplotlib renders as the images the models learn from.
- **NEOS:** each organism's neural network is NumPy weight matrices, and every generation is saved
  as a Matplotlib animation.
- **At BMO:** he cleaned, tagged and augmented the document datasets used to train the extraction
  models.
