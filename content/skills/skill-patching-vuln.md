Edward has led patching and vulnerability management (P&V) at BMO. As Lead Software Developer he led
the team in tracking, planning and patching cloud and application security vulnerabilities across
non-production and production environments, including the DocAI and Lumi products' CVEs, to meet
Cloud, Currency and AppSec compliance standards, reducing open issues by 61%. As Tech Lead he
engineered a proof of concept for an automated vulnerability-patching tool built with Agent Skills
and showcased it to DigitalCore leadership; the tool remediated 98% of open issues.
