Edward has led teams throughout his career. As Tech Lead and Machine Learning Engineer at BMO he
leads a small ML/GenAI and DevOps team on the Apex AI (formerly DigitalCore AI) team. Before that, as
Lead Software Developer, he directed a team of 5 onshore and offshore developers training machine
learning models, delivering 2–6 models per week. He led a cross-functional team of student
entrepreneurs building SpeakEase AI, and an agile team at inQUbate that designed the app's minimum
viable product. He also mentored elementary students as a programming tutor, taking them from no
experience to writing basic programs in one summer.
