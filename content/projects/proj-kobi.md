## Overview
Kobi is the website for a team analysis of Kodi, the open-source multimedia player. Edward worked on
it with Arjun, Raif, Abdul, Danyaal and Drake. The site presents their study of Kodi's
architecture, published at edng5.github.io.

## What it covers
The work was written up in three reports, all linked from the site:
- **Conceptual architecture:** how Kodi's major components and their interactions are designed.
- **Concrete architecture:** how that design is actually realized in Kodi's source code.
- **Enhancements:** proposed improvements to Kodi and how they would fit its architecture.

## Tech
A static website built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages.
