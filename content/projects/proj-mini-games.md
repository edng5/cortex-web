## E.Ngine Games
Ten games playable right here (the PLAY button in the header, or this node's Play button). Six
are Edward's high-school and university Java and Python games, ported to TypeScript; FishMart,
Block Drop, Chess and Four in a Row are new. Each has its own soundtrack.

- **Controls:** W, A, S and D on a computer; an on-screen joystick and buttons on phones.
- **Leaderboards:** signed-in players get global all-time and monthly boards for each game.
- **Saves:** progress is synced to your account, so it's the same on phone and computer.
- **Online:** Chess, Four in a Row and Fishy's multiplayer are played against a friend with a
  6-digit room code (logged-in players only). Each player has a private rating (MMR) that rises
  and falls with wins and losses.

## ![](/images/mini-games/top-gun.jpg) Top Gun
A flight-combat game across three missions (fighter jets, ships, then an army base), earning money
for upgrades to health, missiles, guns and evasive moves.

How to play: W, A, S and D to fly, Space for guns, Shift for a missile, Enter to pause. Each
mission has a leaderboard.

## ![](/games/icons/fishy.png) Fishy
Eat smaller fish to grow and avoid bigger ones. Medium adds sharks, Hard adds fishing hooks, and
beating them unlocks new characters. There's also a two-player mode.

How to play: W, A, S and D to swim (player 2 uses the arrow keys). Each difficulty has a
leaderboard.

## ![](/images/mini-games/space-invaders.jpg) Space Invaders
The arcade classic, with destructible bunkers, a bonus mothership and an unlockable ship. Play on
Normal, Hard (with boss fights) or No Shields (Hard without bunkers).

How to play: A and D to move, Space to shoot, Shift to pause. Each difficulty has a leaderboard.

## ![](/games/icons/frogger-panel.png) Frogger
Edward's Grade 10 pygame game, rebuilt in 3D blocks in the style of Crossy Road.

- **Classic:** cross the road and river to fill all five lily pads before time runs out.
- **Endless:** hop forward through random roads, railways and rivers for as far as you can.
- **Characters:** coins buy gumball-machine rolls that unlock 10 swamp animals.

How to play: W, A, S and D to hop (on phones, tap to hop and swipe to turn). Dodge cars and
trains, and ride logs across the water. Each mode has a leaderboard.

## ![](/games/icons/pong.png) Pong × Brick Breaker
Two paddle games in one. In Pong, the ball speeds up and a second ball joins as the score climbs;
in Brick Breaker, clear all 50 bricks.

How to play: A and D (or the mouse) to move, Space to serve. Each game has a leaderboard.

## ![](/games/icons/ifish.png) FishMart
A fish-store sim made for the site: run an aquarium shop with 800+ real species across seven
rarities, from Common to Mythic. The store keeps running while you're away.

- **Store:** build and stock sale tanks, keep them clean, and expand the floor.
- **Shipments:** random-fish supplier crates, seasonal event crates and free daily gifts.
- **Show tanks:** up to five, decorated and upgraded, plus a partner fish you raise.
- **Staff and research:** hire up to 8; scientists research fish so you can order them.
- **Breeding:** pairs can produce albinos, hybrids and rare mythics.
- **Fishing:** keep the bar on the fish until the catch meter fills.
- **Market:** trade fish with other players and visit their stores in the global showcase.

## ![](/images/mini-games/i-spy.jpg) iSpy
Edward's I Spy (Python and OpenCV: the computer guessed an object from its colour), made into an
arcade game.

- **Seek & Find:** "I spy with my little eye, something red…": click a match in a busy scene
  before time runs out. Quick finds build a combo; later levels add lookalikes and clues like
  "something green you can eat".
- **Stump the Computer:** think of something in the scene, say its colour, and the computer gets
  three guesses.

## ![](/games/icons/blockdrop.png) Block Drop
An original falling-block puzzle game: fill rows to clear them; every 10 rows the level goes up
and the pieces fall faster. On the global leaderboards.

## ![](/games/icons/chess.png) Chess and Four in a Row
Played online against a friend with a room code. The server checks every move, so ratings can't
be cheated. Pick a board theme; resign, offer a draw (chess) or ask for a rematch.
